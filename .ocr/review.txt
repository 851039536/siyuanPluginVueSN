also warm it up conditionally on the feature flag and route teardown to

1. **Unload leak / WAL not merged:** `BrowserManager.destroy()`
(src/features/minimalBrowser/types/index.ts:185-188) only removes the listener and unmounts the

with (1) — leaves the handle open forever. `registerWebsiteNavigation` and `registerMinimalBrowser`
are independent flags, so let each registration decide whether it needs the store, or move the
warmup to the plugin level guarded by `enableWebsiteNavigation || enableMinimalBrowser`.

returns `null` for **any** internal failure — not just an unresolvable path. A hook-side error such
